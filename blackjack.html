HOW TO PLAY · BLACKJACK 21

1. Objective: Beat the Dealer's hand total without exceeding 21. Going over 21 is a Bust (automatic loss).
2. Card Values: Number cards (2–10) count as face value. Face cards (J, Q, K) count as 10. Aces (A) count as 11 or 1 (automatically adjusted so you never bust unnecessarily).
3. Natural Blackjack (3:2): An Ace + 10-value card on your first two cards pays 3:2 (1.5x your bet), unless the Dealer also has Blackjack (Push — your bet is returned).
4. Special Moves:
• Double Down: Double your wager on your initial 2 cards to receive exactly one final card.
• Split Pairs: If your first 2 cards have equal value, split them into two independent hands.
• Insurance: When the Dealer shows an Ace, wager half your bet that the Dealer has a 10 underneath (pays 2:1).
5. Dealer Rules & Shortcuts: Dealer draws until reaching 17 or higher and stands on all 17s.
